INSIDE DAYLIGHT

A useful forecast.
A living companion.

Know your sky
Live conditions, feels-like temperature, wind, precipitation, humidity, and the hours ahead. Use approximate location or search for a city.
Watch the world change
The scene follows the weather and local daylight. Sunrise, sunset, rain, and night give your companion a changing backdrop.
Take a little company along
Optional motion and place scenes let your companion ride, walk, browse, or take a café break alongside you.
Make it your own
Choose Botanical, Robot, or Minimal orb. Explore accelerated weather and progression scenarios in the isolated simulator.

DATA & PERMISSIONS

Your world, on your terms.

No account is required. Weather uses an optional approximate location or a city you choose. Motion and precise place detection are separate opt-in features.

Weather coordinates and city searches go to Open-Meteo. Optional place queries use Google. Progress and settings are saved on your device; uninstalling clears them.

Live growth uses fresh weather and motion evidence while open at your current location. City selections are previews; time away isn't guessed.

New to APKs?

These are direct Android installs. Download the file, open it, and follow your phone's prompts. Future updates are installed manually over the existing app; keep your app data by avoiding uninstalling between updates.
