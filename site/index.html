DATA & PERMISSIONS

Your world, on your terms.

No account is required. Weather uses an optional approximate location or a city you choose. Motion and precise place detection are separate opt-in features.

FOUR WAYS IN

Make room for the useful parts.

01

Summarise

Bring pasted text or a text file. Ask for a brief, key points or explicitly stated action items.

02

Write

Continue a draft, polish your wording or say it in fewer words. You stay in charge of the final version.

03

See

Choose an image for a description, a caption or a question about what's visible.

04

Listen

Try English transcription or a voice summary for recordings up to 20 seconds. An experiment for short clips; live captions and long recordings come later.

LOCAL BY DESIGN

Your workspace.
Your device.

Download Gemma 4 E2B once from the Model screen. Text and media are then processed on device, with no account or cloud inference service. Microphone access is requested only when you choose Record.

A little preparation

The model is a separate 2.59 GB download; allow at least 3 GB free storage. A device with 6–8 GB RAM is recommended. Android 8.0+ and ARM64 or x86-64 are required; speed and compatibility vary by device.

Keep what matters

Save completed results locally, or copy, share and export text. AI can miss details or invent information: review every result before relying on it.

THREE LITTLE PERSONALITIES

Choose your kind of zoom.

PLAY AT YOUR OWN PACE

Your little arena.
Always close by.

Play entirely offline against built-in bots, with no account, ads, currency or paid unlocks. No online multiplayer or cloud service is required.

Touch, steer, boost

A floating joystick appears where you touch. Pull to steer, then double-tap and hold to boost. Prefer a fixed joystick? Settings offers a separate boost button too.

A pause when you need it

The game pauses in the background and needs a tap to resume. Completed records and characters are saved locally; active runs aren't recovered after Android closes the app. Uninstalling clears local data.

ROOM FOR YOUR NEXT IDEA

A little melody.
A whole arrangement.

Build a beat, record a phrase, and layer your instruments into a song. Mozart brings a piano roll, an expressive playing surface and a pattern-based timeline into an offline studio made for tablets and adapted for phones.

Inside the studio ↓
Studio illustration, not a device screenshot

FROM FIRST NOTE TO FINAL MIX

Make room for the music.

01 / COMPOSE

Catch the phrase.

Play a two-octave keyboard or twelve drum pads, record held notes and chords, or write directly into a piano roll. Keys, bass, warm pad, pluck and drums use original sounds.

02 / ARRANGE

Give it a shape.

Layer up to twelve instruments across a 64-bar timeline. Reuse linked patterns, create variations and name your song sections.

03 / PERFORM

Keep it moving.

Play in full-screen Live mode with octave controls, pitch bend, chord pads and an arpeggiator. Cue sections and jump at the next bar boundary.

04 / MIX & SHARE

Make it your own.

Automate tracks, balance a stereo mix and add room or tempo-synced delay. Export WAV, MIDI, stems or a portable project bundle.

YOUR MUSIC STAYS WITH YOU

An offline studio.
An open-ended idea.

No account, ads, analytics or cloud service. Projects, sounds and recovery copies stay on your device. Share files only when you choose an export or the Android share menu.

Made for a little more space

Wide tablets show arrangement and editing side by side. Portrait and smaller windows adapt to one workspace, with a full-screen keyboard or drum pads in Live mode.

Keep a copy

Export important projects before uninstalling or replacing a development build. Uninstalling clears local data. External hardware MIDI, microphone/audio recording and EQ are not included in this release.
